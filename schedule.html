Development Schedule

This schedule shows the major stages of CRAB CAKES development and project milestones throughout Fall 2026.

Current Focus

PIT Crew is currently preparing for the November Design Review while developing and refining prototypes for CRAB CAKES v2. Our recent work has focused on QA/QC features, including graphs, filters, pivot tables, and summaries generated from locally stored fish survey data. We are also continuing work on Bluetooth scanner connectivity and reviewing project requirements with our sponsors.

September

Project Familiarization

Meet with sponsors to learn how the project will be used in the field and understand their expectations for the system. Review and become familiar with Shoals and the previous CRAB CAKES version.

Milestones & Deliverables

  • SICCS Engineering Fest Design Review, November 20
  • Engineering Fest Poster
  • Requirements Document

October

Technical Feasibility

Investigate the existing codebase, Bluetooth scanner connectivity, Linux support, GUI improvements, and other technical options. Begin working with PIT-tag hardware when it becomes available.

Milestones & Deliverables

  • Technical Feasibility Document

November

Prototype Development

Develop and refine prototypes for CRAB CAKES v2, with a focus on QA/QC visualization, database integration, and improved field workflows. Review requirements with sponsors and prepare the prototypes, documentation, and presentation for the November Design Review.

Milestones & Deliverables

  • SICCS Engineering Fest Design Review, November 20
  • Engineering Fest Poster
  • Requirements Document

December

Technical Demonstration

Continue developing and testing the prototype and demonstrate the team's technical progress. Finalize project requirements and prepare the project for the next stage of development.

Milestones & Deliverables

  • Technical Demo
  • Marketing Video
  • Fish Cooperators Meeting

Resources

PIT Crew is continuing development of CRAB CAKES, originally created by the previous NAU Capstone team, SharkBytes. Their project website and source code provide a starting point for our work.

  • SharkBytes Project Website (CRAB CAKES v1)
  • CRAB CAKES v1 GitHub Repository
  • Original Sponsor Project Description

Our sponsors also provide access to the SHOALS application, PIT-tag scanners, and test tags for development and testing. Sponsor meetings help guide our requirements and ensure the application meets the needs of field biologists.
