Development

Technologies

CRAB CAKES v2 is a desktop application developed using Flutter and Dart. Our team is continuing the work of the previous NAU Capstone team to improve how biologists collect and manage fish survey data in the field.

The application uses a local SQLite database to store fish survey records, allowing biologists to work without an internet connection. We are also using the Flutter package fl_chart to create graphs and summaries for QA/QC.

Our development focuses on improving Bluetooth PIT-tag scanner connectivity, expanding compatibility to Windows and Linux, and making the application easier to use during field surveys.
