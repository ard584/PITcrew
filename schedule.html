Development Schedule

This schedule shows the major stages of CRAB CAKES development and project milestones throughout Fall 2026.

Current Focus

PIT Crew is currently moving into technical feasibility work, which includes reviewing the existing codebase, learning more about Bluetooth scanner connectivity, and investigating possible improvements to the interface and system compatibility.

September

Project Familiarization

Meet with sponsors to learn how the project will be used in the field and understand their expectations for the system. Review and become familiar with Shoals and the previous CRAB CAKES version.

Milestones & Deliverables

  • Team Inventory
  • Mini-Intro Presentation, September 25
  • Team Standards

October

Technical Feasibility

Investigate the existing codebase, Bluetooth scanner connectivity, Linux support, GUI improvements, and other technical options. Begin working with PIT-tag hardware when it becomes available.

Milestones & Deliverables

  • Technical Feasibility Document

November

Prototype Development

Continue prioritizing requirements with the sponsors and begin building and refining prototypes for the most important parts of the system. Use these prototypes to test design ideas and important functionality.

Milestones & Deliverables

  • SICCS Engineering FEST Design Review Presentation, November 20
  • Requirements Document

December

Technical Demonstration

Continue developing and testing the prototype and demonstrate the team's technical progress. Finalize project requirements and prepare the project for the next stage of development.

Milestones & Deliverables

  • Technical Demo
  • Marketing Video
  • Fish Cooperators Meeting

Resources

PIT Crew is building on the existing CRAB CAKES codebase and documentation created by SharkBytes, the previous Capstone team. PIT Crew also has access to Shoals so the current field workflow and existing features can be reviewed throughout development.

A PIT-tag scanner and test PIT tags will be provided for development and testing. Sponsor meetings also provide guidance on field requirements, technical needs, and how the program is currently used.

Development will use software and tools that are freely available online.
