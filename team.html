Meet PIT Crew

PIT Crew is made up of Luke Flaker, Mia Gallo, and Annaliese Dedmore, with the team name inspired by the PIT tags used to identify and track fish during monitoring. The team is continuing the development of CRAB CAKES for fish monitoring in the Colorado River and Grand Canyon.
