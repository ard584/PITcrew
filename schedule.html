Development Schedule

Major stages and milestones for CRAB CAKES v2 throughout Fall 2026.

Current Focus

PIT Crew is preparing for the November Design Review and refining prototypes. Recent work includes QA/QC graphs, filters, pivot tables, and summaries generated from locally stored fish survey data. We are also continuing work on scanner connectivity and reviewing requirements with our sponsors.

September

Project Familiarization

Meet with sponsors, learn about field survey workflows, and review SHOALS and CRAB CAKES v1.

Milestones & Deliverables

  • Team Inventory
  • Mini Presentation
  • Team Standards

October

Technical Feasibility

Research Linux support, Bluetooth connectivity, GUI improvements, QA/QC tools, and the feasibility of the existing application.

Milestones & Deliverables

  • Technical Feasibility Document

Prototype Development

Refine QA/QC visualizations and database integration, review requirements with sponsors, and prepare for the Design Review.

Milestones & Deliverables
